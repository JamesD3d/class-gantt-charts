# Master 18-Week Semester Pacing Guide

## Continuous Shop Operations
Standards: Obj 7.01, 7.02, 2.06, 6.01, 6.02
Start: 1
Duration: 18
Label: Shop Ops & Portfolios
Tooltip: Safety, Management, & Weekly Portfolio Updates
- Daily student-led **Industrial Shop Management Rotations** for safety and tool organization
- Maintain the weekly digital **Technical Documentation & Evidence Portfolio**
- Explore CTE pathways and compile the **CTSO / TSA Career & Leadership Portfolios**
- Practice durable employability skills like **collaboration**, **adaptability**, and **personal responsibility**

## 2D Design & Laser Cutting
Standards: Obj 5.01, 5.02
Start: 1
Duration: 2
Label: 2D CAD & Laser Cut
Tooltip: Logo Trace & Keychain Engraving
- Master basic **CAD commands** (line, circle, arc, polyline, fillet, chamfer)
- Complete the **Logo Trace** to practice sketch geometry and vector segment alignment
- Utilize **subtractive modeling** and geometric **hatch patterns** to design a custom **Keychain**
- Safely operate the laser cutter to engrave and cut the physical keychain model

## 3D Parametric Modeling
Standards: Obj 5.02
Start: 3
Duration: 2
Label: 3D CAD & Printing
Tooltip: Eyeglasses & Cell Phone Holders
- Use geometric **constraints** and **parametric variables** to draft standard parts
- Model custom **Eyeglasses** balancing human ergonomics, comfort, and usability
- Design a parametric **Cell Phone Holder** balancing physical form, function, and visual weight
- Export finished .STL files to the instructor's master 3D printing queue

## Drawings & Printing Catch-Up
Standards: Obj 5.02, 5.03
Start: 5
Duration: 2
Label: Drawings & Printing
Tooltip: Copy Object & Structural Hook Optimization
- Reverse-engineer a real object for the **Copy Object Model Project**
- Create annotated sketches and formal, dimensioned **orthographic projection** drawing sheets
- Use CAD **fillet tools** on a **Structural Hook** to smooth high-stress interior corners
- Run 3D printers 24/7 in the background to complete student phone holders and hooks

## Materials & Destructive Testing
Standards: Obj 2.03, 3.03
Start: 7
Duration: 1
Label: Material Strength
Tooltip: Paper Bridge & Compression Testing
- Study physical material properties including **compression strength** and **tensile strength**
- Evaluate trade-offs between project **criteria** (goals) and **constraints** (material limits)
- Build and crush paper bridge structures to calculate load-to-weight structural efficiency
- Log test data and document material failures in your digital evidence portfolio

## Simple Machines Heavy Build
Standards: Obj 4.03
Start: 8
Duration: 2
Label: Cargo Crane Build
Tooltip: Precision Crane & Mechanical Advantage
- Construct a physical **Precision Cargo Crane** utilizing levers and pulleys
- Practice structural mechanics, mechanical work, and force equations
- Complete worksheets proving theoretical **Ideal Mechanical Advantage (IMA)**
- Measure and record **Actual Mechanical Advantage (AMA)** under physical loading

## Aerodynamics Lab
Standards: Obj 3.05, 2.01
Start: 10
Duration: 1
Label: Aerodynamics
Tooltip: Transportation Profiling & Launch Tests
- Study transportation system principles, aerodynamics, and fluid resistance
- Model and 3D print a custom **Transportation Aerodynamic Profile (Airfoil)**
- Launch airfoils using compressed air cannons and record flight velocity
- Analyze aerodynamic performance logs and compile experimental findings

## Concrete Mold & Pour
Standards: Obj 2.05, 3.02, 3.03
Start: 11
Duration: 2
Label: Concrete Casting
Tooltip: Mold Design & Volumetric Calculations
- Design a custom parametric mold in CAD for **Cast Concrete Desk Organizers**
- Compute three-dimensional **volume**, area, and surface area mathematical models
- Assemble the structural mold and physically pour the liquid concrete mix
- Allow concrete to cure undisturbed for a 48-hour cycle (overlap with solder boxes)

## Sheet Metal & Soldering
Standards: Obj 2.02, 3.02
Start: 12
Duration: 2
Label: Metal Soldering
Tooltip: Copper Solder Box Layout & Assembly
- Review lab safety, **PPE** requirements, and classroom hazard communication procedures
- Perform safe sheet metal layouts, shearing, bending, and brake forming
- Fabricate a physical **Copper Solder Box** using thermal **joining** (soldering)
- Evaluate physical metal properties including **ductility** and **malleability**

## Systems & Electronics Capstone
Standards: Obj 4.01, 4.02, 4.04, 3.06
Start: 14
Duration: 2
Label: IoT Smart Garden
Tooltip: Breadboard Wiring, Sensors, & Ohm's Law
- Wire automated electronic circuits on a reusable, solderless breadboard
- Study the **Universal Systems Model** components (Input, Process, Output, Feedback)
- Connect moisture, temperature, and pump sensors to microcontroller pins
- Apply **Ohm's Law** calculations to protect electronic semiconductor components

## Thermodynamics & Energy
Standards: Obj 3.01
Start: 16
Duration: 1
Label: Energy Racers
Tooltip: Rubber-Band Cars & Energy Flow
- Build and test high-speed **Energy-Conversion Rubber-Band Cars**
- Apply the laws of thermodynamics (conservation of energy and entropy increases)
- Construct a **Sankey diagram** to map kinetic potential, work, and waste heat
- Perform racing trials to evaluate the efficiency of physical energy transfer

## VEX Robotics & Coding
Standards: Std 1.00
Start: 17
Duration: 1
Label: Robotics Coding
Tooltip: Block Programming & Sensor Logic
- Write **block-based code** statements to navigate the VEX robotics chassis
- Define data variables (**Boolean, float, integer, string**) and function parameters
- Program logical control loops and conditional **If/Else** statements
- Interface on-board accelerometer and gyroscope sensors to guide robots autonomously

Standards: Standard 2.00, 5.00, 6.00, 7.00

Duration: 1
Label: CTE Final Exam

- Finalize and export all digital portfolio deliverables and student self-checks
- Review the complete master vocabulary definitions across all 18 mini-units
